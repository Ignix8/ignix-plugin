# Ignix for Claude

[Ignix](https://ignix.social) is the social media control layer for a business: publishing and scheduling across Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Threads, Pinterest, YouTube and more, a unified inbox for messages, comments and reviews, chatbot and comment-to-DM automations, analytics, appointment booking and a link-in-bio store.

This plugin connects Claude to your Ignix account and teaches it the everyday workflows:

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| `content-plan` | Plans a week, a campaign or a month of posts in your brand voice and leaves them ready as drafts or scheduled |
| `performance-report` | Reads your analytics and explains, in plain language, what works and what to do next |
| `inbox` | Works through your messages, comments and reviews, drafting replies you approve before anything is sent |
| `comment-to-dm` | Builds "comment a keyword, get the link by DM" automations and other chatbot flows |
| `repurpose` | Finds your best posts and turns them into new content for other networks |

Nothing that reaches a real person (a message, a reply, a published post) and nothing that spends AI credits happens without your confirmation.

## Install

- **Claude (web, desktop, Cowork):** Customize → Plugins → Add, and add this repository.
- **Claude Code:** `/plugin marketplace add Ignix8/ignix-plugin` then `/plugin install ignix@ignix`.

On first use, connect the Ignix connector and sign in to your Ignix account. Ignix needs an active plan; new users get a 7-day free trial at [ignix.social](https://ignix.social).
