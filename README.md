# Ignix for Claude

[Ignix](https://ignix.social) is an all-in-one social media manager for creators, small businesses and agencies. This plugin connects Claude to your Ignix account and adds ready-made workflows, so you can plan, publish, answer and measure your social media in plain words.

**Social media scheduler.** Plan and schedule posts, carousels, Reels and Stories on 13 networks (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Threads, Pinterest, Bluesky, Reddit, Google Business Profile, Telegram and Discord) from one content calendar, with a posting queue and the best times to post.

**AI content in your brand voice.** Captions, hashtags, post ideas, images and a full month of drafts based on what works in your niche. Turn your best posts into new content for other networks.

**Instagram DM automation.** Comment-to-DM (send a link by DM when someone comments a keyword), story replies, lead capture and chatbot flows for Instagram, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, Telegram and website live chat, plus broadcasts and drip sequences.

**Unified inbox.** DMs, comments, mentions and reviews from every account in one place, with a CRM, tags, team assignment and approvals. Claude drafts the replies; you approve them.

**Social media analytics.** Reach, engagement, follower growth and top posts per network, explained in plain language, with a shareable client report and social listening for brand mentions and sentiment.

**Competitor and niche research.** Analyze any public Instagram, TikTok or YouTube account, see which posts beat its usual numbers and find the top reels in your niche.

**Also included:** a link-in-bio store for digital products, courses and memberships with 0% Ignix commission, appointment booking synced to Google Calendar, and one workspace per client for agencies.

Nothing is published, sent or charged without your OK.

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| `content-plan` | Plans a week, a campaign or a month of posts in your brand voice and leaves them ready as drafts or scheduled |
| `performance-report` | Reads your analytics and explains, in plain language, what works and what to do next |
| `inbox` | Works through your messages, comments and reviews, drafting replies you approve before anything is sent |
| `comment-to-dm` | Builds "comment a keyword, get the link by DM" automations and other chatbot flows |
| `repurpose` | Finds your best posts and turns them into new content for other networks |

## How to use

Ask Claude things like:

- "Plan next week's Instagram and TikTok posts for my coffee shop and schedule them at the best times."
- "When someone comments GUIDE on my next post, DM them the link to my free guide."
- "How did my accounts perform this month? Which post brought the most followers?"
- "Show me unread DMs and comments from all my accounts and draft replies."
- "Analyze @competitor on Instagram and tell me which of their posts beat their usual numbers."
- "Turn my three best LinkedIn posts into an Instagram carousel."

## Install

- **Claude (web, desktop, Cowork):** Customize → Plugins → Add, and add this repository.
- **Claude Code:** `/plugin marketplace add Ignix8/ignix-plugin` then `/plugin install ignix@ignix`.

On first use, connect the Ignix connector and sign in to your Ignix account. Ignix needs an active plan; new users get a 7-day free trial at [ignix.social](https://ignix.social). To publish, use the inbox or see analytics, connect at least one social account in Ignix; Instagram automations need an Instagram professional (Business or Creator) account.
